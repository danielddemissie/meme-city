# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Desktop-browser players and streamers attempting short, tense, replayable survival-horror escape runs lasting roughly 5–10 minutes. They need immediate controls, fast restarts, clear threat feedback, recognizable meme staff, customizable audio, and escalating pressure without a long onboarding process.

## Product Purpose

Meme City: Brainrot Escape turns absurd internet lore into a deadpan bureaucratic nightmare. Each run asks players to navigate a hostile municipal space, understand and exploit enemy rules, recover the required credentials, and escape. Success means a complete escape; the game should make failure legible and restarting immediate.

## Current Slice

Six staff members share the observation rule while varying speed, focus pressure, movement sound, and administrative flavor: the Glancing Man, Sr. Tung Tung Tung, Erwin Tralalero, Capt. Bombardiro, Ballerina C., and Groundskeeper Patapim. The five brainrot renders are sourced from Wikimedia Commons as public-domain assets; source provenance ships in `public/assets/SOURCES.txt`.

Synth audio provides movement cues, detection, focus warnings, blinks, and outcomes without bundling meme recordings. Players and streamers can replace these cues at runtime by dropping named MP3, WAV, or OGG files onto the game. URL staff selection supports repeatable clips and QA.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, oppressive, absurd. Institutional rules and administrative language treat brainrot phenomena as routine public hazards. The horror comes from procedure, surveillance, and enemies that obey unsettling rules—not from cartoonish meme comedy or constant jump scares.

## Anti-references

Do not use cartoon meme collage, comedy-game presentation, grungy VHS as a blanket aesthetic, familiar horror-game HUD clichés, excessive jump scares, or glitch effects that obscure play. Avoid decorative interface complexity that competes with spatial awareness.

## Design Principles

- Make every threat understandable through observation and consistent rules.
- Keep administrative language deadpan while the underlying situation remains absurd.
- Build tension through restricted information, restrained audio and visual cues, and escalating enemy behavior rather than surprise alone.
- Keep the playfield dominant; interface elements should clarify immediate decisions and otherwise recede.
- Make failure quick to read and restarting frictionless.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide readable contrast, complete keyboard controls, reduced-motion support, and no color-only gameplay cues. Include independent controls for enemy pressure, screen effects, flash intensity, and audio so players can tune the horror experience without losing the core rules.
